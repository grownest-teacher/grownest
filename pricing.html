Questions schools ask

Is there a free trial?

We offer a guided demo, and our founding schools receive a special first-year offer. Ask us about it when you book your demo.

Do teachers need an account?

No. Only observers and school leaders sign in. Teachers receive their feedback by email and reply with one tap.

How is our data kept private?

Each school has its own private space, and no school can see another school’s teachers or observations. Photo evidence is stored privately and only your school’s staff can view it.

How do we pay?

Plans are billed once a year by invoice, in UAE dirhams.

What happens if our school grows?

You can move to a larger plan at any time, and we adjust the price for the rest of the year.

Can we take our data with us?

Yes. Teacher lists download as Excel, and observations, appraisals and SEF evidence download as Word documents at any time.
