What’s inside

Everything school leaders need for quality assurance

Learning walks

Short visits focused on chosen MOE indicators, marked seen, partly seen or not yet seen, with lesson stage and time.

Formal observations

All 12 lesson-observable MOE indicators on the six-point scale, with a bank of feedback statements for every level.

Appraisals

Termly or annual appraisals with ratings suggested from the year’s observation evidence and agreed targets.

Teacher records

One page per teacher with a timeline, growth chart, indicator profile and a downloadable summary.

Photo evidence

Take photos during the lesson, link each one to an indicator and keep it privately with the record.

Word forms

Professional observation and appraisal forms, ready to sign, file or attach.

Meeting requests

See at a glance which teachers asked to meet about their feedback, and mark meetings as done.

Private by school

Each school has its own secure space. Teachers and data are never shared between schools.

Built around the UAE framework

Aligned to the MOE inspection framework

Every judgement uses the six-point MOE scale, and every indicator carries its framework reference, so your evidence is ready for self-evaluation and inspection.
